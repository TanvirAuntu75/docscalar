Section One

Executive Summary

Fiscal year 2025 marked a defining inflection point for TechVision. Against a backdrop of macroeconomic uncertainty, tightening credit conditions, and accelerating AI commoditisation, the company posted record revenues of $2.34 billion, representing a year-on-year growth rate of 31.4%. EBITDA margins expanded by 280 basis points to 24.6%, driven by disciplined cost management and a favourable shift in product mix toward high-margin enterprise subscriptions.

Our cloud infrastructure division surpassed the $1 billion annual recurring revenue threshold for the first time, while the newly launched Nexus AI platform attracted over 4,800 enterprise customers in its first six months of general availability. Headcount grew 18% to 9,240 full-time employees across 23 countries.

Section Seven

2026 Strategic Outlook

FY2026 Guidance (Preliminary)

Management expects total revenue in the range of $3.05B–$3.20B, representing growth of 30–37% year-over-year. EBITDA margin is guided at 26–28%, reflecting continued operating leverage offset by planned headcount growth of ~22% as we accelerate the Nexus AI go-to-market.

  • Cloud ARR target: $1.55B
  • Nexus AI ARR target: $620M
  • New AZ openings: Tokyo, São Paulo
  • Headcount target: ~11,300
  • R&D investment: $480M
  • M&A budget: $250M reserved

Strategic Priorities

AI-First Product Architecture
Embed Nexus AI capabilities natively into VisionCloud, DataLens, and FlowOps — eliminating the need for separate AI licensing in bundled tiers.
APAC Market Expansion
Establish direct sales presence in Japan, South Korea, and Australia. Localise Nexus AI for Japanese and Korean enterprise requirements.
Sovereign Cloud Programme
Launch dedicated EU data-residency infrastructure to address GDPR and Digital Markets Act compliance requirements for regulated sectors.
